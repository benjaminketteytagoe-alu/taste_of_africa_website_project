Rwandan Isombe

⏱ Prep: 30 min🍳 Cook: 1 hrMedium

Servings: 4-6 people

Cultural History & Significance

Isombe is a traditional Rwandan dish made from pounded cassava leaves cooked in a rich peanut sauce. It is a staple of Central African cuisine and represents the agricultural heritage of the Great Lakes region. The dish is often served at family gatherings and celebrations, accompanied by plantains or rice.

Cooking Steps

Phase 1: Ingredient Prep & Marinade
  1. Wash cassava leaves thoroughly and remove tough stems.
  2. Chop the leaves finely.
  3. Roast and grind peanuts into a coarse paste.
  4. Chop onions and mince garlic.
Phase 2: Sauce Base & Browning
  1. Heat oil in a large pot. Sauté onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the peanut paste and stir to combine with the onions.
Phase 3: Simmering & Slow Cooking
  1. Add the chopped cassava leaves to the pot.
  2. Pour in enough water or stock to cover the leaves.
  3. Simmer for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the leaves are tender and the sauce is thick.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, and a touch of palm oil for color.
Phase 4: Plating & Traditional Accompaniments
  1. Transfer the isombe to a serving dish.
  2. Serve with boiled plantains, rice, or ugali.
  3. Garnish with crushed roasted peanuts.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg fresh cassava leaves
  • 1 cup roasted peanuts
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/4 cup palm oil
  • 2 cups water or stock
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Vegetable oil for cooking

Equipment

  1. Large pot with lid
  2. Mortar and pestle or blender
  3. Sharp knife and cutting board
  4. Wooden spoon
