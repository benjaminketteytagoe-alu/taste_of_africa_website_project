Ethiopian Injera & Doro Wat

⏱ Prep: 45 min🍳 Cook: 1.5 hrsHard

Servings: 4-6 people

Cultural History & Significance

Injera is the staple food of Ethiopia and Eritrea, a spongy flatbread made from fermented teff flour. Doro Wat, a spicy chicken stew, is considered the national dish and is served on special occasions. The combination represents the heart of Ethiopian communal dining, where food is shared from a large platter of injera topped with various wats (stews).

Cooking Steps

Phase 1: Ingredient Prep & Marinade
  1. Prepare the injera batter by mixing teff flour with water and letting it ferment for 2-3 days.
  2. Clean and cut chicken into pieces. Marinate with berbere spice, garlic, ginger, and lemon juice for 1 hour.
  3. Hard-boil 6 eggs and peel them.
  4. Prepare niter kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced butter) by simmering butter with onions, garlic, and spices.
Phase 2: Sauce Base & Browning
  1. In a large pot, heat niter kibbeh and sauté chopped onions until deeply caramelized, about 20 minutes.
  2. Add the marinated chicken and cook until browned on all sides.
  3. Add berbere spice paste and stir to coat the chicken evenly.
Phase 3: Simmering & Slow Cooking
  1. Pour in chicken stock and bring to a boil.
  2. Reduce heat and simmer for 45 minutes until chicken is tender and sauce is thick.
  3. Nestle the hard-boiled eggs into the stew and cook for another 15 minutes.
Phase 4: Plating & Traditional Accompaniments
  1. Cook the injera on a hot griddle until bubbly and set, about 3-4 minutes per side.
  2. Place a large round of injera on a serving platter.
  3. Spoon the doro wat over the injera, ensuring each portion has chicken and egg.
  4. Serve with additional injera on the side for scooping.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 4 cups teff flour (for injera)
  • 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
  • 6 hard-boiled eggs
  • 1 cup berbere spice blend
  • 1/2 cup niter kibbeh (spiced butter)
  • 3 large onions, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 inches ginger
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Equipment

  1. Large pot with lid
  2. Flat griddle or injera pan
  3. Blender for spice paste
  4. Serving platter
