Moroccan Lamb & Apricot Tagine

⏱ Prep: 30 min🍳 Cook: 2 hrsHard

Servings: 4-6 people

Cultural History & Significance

Tagine is Morocco's most iconic dish, named after the conical clay pot in which it is cooked. The slow-cooking method allows the meat to become incredibly tender while the flavors meld together beautifully. This lamb and apricot version represents the sweet-savory balance that defines Moroccan cuisine, influenced by Berber, Arab, and Mediterranean traditions.

Cooking Steps

Phase 1: Ingredient Prep & Marinade
  1. Cut lamb shoulder into 2-inch cubes and pat dry.
  2. In a bowl, mix cumin, cinnamon, ginger, turmeric, saffron, salt, and pepper.
  3. Coat the lamb cubes evenly with the spice mixture and let marinate for 30 minutes.
  4. Soak dried apricots in warm water for 15 minutes, then drain.
Phase 2: Sauce Base & Browning
  1. Heat olive oil in a tagine or heavy pot. Brown the lamb on all sides, about 8 minutes.
  2. Add chopped onions and garlic, cook until softened.
  3. Add honey, lemon juice, and chicken stock. Bring to a simmer.
Phase 3: Simmering & Slow Cooking
  1. Cover and cook on low heat for 1.5 hours until lamb is tender.
  2. Add the soaked apricots and continue cooking for 20 minutes.
  3. Stir in chopped almonds and fresh cilantro.
Phase 4: Plating & Traditional Accompaniments
  1. Serve over fluffy couscous.
  2. Garnish with toasted almonds, fresh cilantro, and a drizzle of honey.
  3. Accompany with a side of preserved lemons and harissa.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 1 cup dried apricots
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • Saffron threads
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1/2 cup almonds
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Olive oil

Equipment

  1. Tagine pot or heavy Dutch oven
  2. Sharp knife and cutting board
  3. Measuring spoons
  4. Wooden spoon
